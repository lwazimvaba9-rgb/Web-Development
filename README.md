1. Organisation Overview 
•Mandarin Motors is a Durban-based motor-parts business that supplies vehicle and 
motorcycle-related parts and accessories. 
• Public business directories list the organisation at 163 West Street, Durban Central, and 
classify it as a motor-parts store. One directory lists the telephone number as 031 335 
0100. For this academic project. 
•The organisation’s proposed mission is: “To provide reliable bike parts, accessories and 
maintenance support at fair prices.” Its proposed vision is: “To become a trusted and 
accessible bike-parts supplier for riders and repair businesses in KwaZulu-Natal.” 
The target audience includes: -Motorcycle and scooter owners. -Bicycle or motorbike repair enthusiasts. -Delivery riders. -Small repair workshops. -Customers looking for replacement parts and accessories. 
2. Website Goals and Objectives 
The proposed website will create a professional online presence and make it easier for 
customers to find products, request assistance and contact the business. 
The main objectives; 
•Display available bike parts, accessories and services. 
•Generate enquiries through an online form. 
•Provide accurate business hours, contact details and directions. 
•Encourage customers to request a quotation before visiting the store. 
•Improve customer confidence through clear product information and photographs. 
•The key performance indicators will include: 
•At least 100 website visitors during the first three months. 
•At least 20 completed product or repair enquiries per month. 
•An enquiry-form completion rate of at least 5%. 
•At least 10 telephone or WhatsApp enquiries generated by the website each month. 
•A reduction in repeated customer questions about location, opening hours and available 
services. 
3. Current Website Analysis 
No verified official Mandarin Motors website was identified during the initial research. This 
presents an opportunity to create a central online information source. Existing directory 
listings may help customers find the business, but they provide limited information about 
products, services, repairs and enquiry procedures. 
The new website should improve the organisation’s digital presence by including: 
•A clear homepage and value proposition. 
•A searchable or categorised product section. 
•A service and repair page. 
•A quotation/enquiry form. 
•Accurate contact information. 
•Mobile-friendly navigation. 
4. Proposed Features 
The website will contain: 
•Homepage with a hero image, introduction and call-to-action buttons. 
•About Us page containing the business history, mission and values. 
•Products page containing categories such as oils, chains, brake parts, tyres, batteries and 
accessories. 
•Services page describing inspections, part fitting, maintenance and basic repairs. 
•Enquiry page with fields for the customer’s name, contact details, bike model, required 
part and message. 
•Contact page containing telephone, email, operating hours, social-media links and two 
map locations. 
•Frequently Asked Questions section. 
•Responsive design for smartphones, tablets and desktop computers.
